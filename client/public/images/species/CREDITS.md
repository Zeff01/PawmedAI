# Species photographs

One photograph per species for the case library’s patient tile, used by
`client/src/features/student/caseMeta.ts`. Each is the lead image of the
linked English Wikipedia article, taken from Wikimedia Commons under the
licence shown, then centre-cropped to 4:3 and saved at 480×360.

These are stock photographs of the species, not the patients written up in
the cases — the cases are teaching simulations.

## Replacing or adding one

Pick a freely licensed photograph of the species (Wikimedia Commons is the
source used here — check the file page says public domain or a CC licence,
and avoid the montage images some articles use as their lead), crop it to
4:3 at 480×360, save it as `<species>.jpg` beside these, and add a row
above with the author, licence and source page. The species itself is
declared in `Case.Species` (server) and `SPECIES_META` (client).
